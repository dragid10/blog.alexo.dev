## What's going on with Alex?

Things have been pretty nice lately. I was in Philadelphia for a wedding this past weekend. It was wonderful! Philly is a very nice place, and I would absolutely go back.

I just completed maintenance on my home lab. I've had 6 TB drives that I needed to throw in my server for quite a while now, but it required downtime, which sucks when I want to watch Plex. I did the maintenance this weekend finally because my storage was sitting at 95% usage, so I figured that I might as well just do it. This maintenance itself was expected to take like four or five days, but it completely wrapped in about two days, fortunately.

It was just three drives that I was replacing, so you have to go through the process of taking out the old drive, slotting in the new drive, and letting it resilver completely. That's typically the part that takes the time, the hours really. Then you do a replication to your backup drive and start the process again with the next drive. I'm using RAID Z2 with six drives, so I can't just swap in drives all at once, or else I risk losing the whole storage or data set, which is bad.

I finally got a haircut. I've been complaining about my hair for about a month or two now, so getting a haircut was nice.

### Apple Health

I've been exploring iOS for the first time in years. I've always found it pretty confusing to use, but I wanted to revisit my biases against it. My opinion largely remains the same, but something I really liked with iOS is actually Apple Health. I believe it's a really good app, I liked its features and functionality, and I like how straightforward it was.

I think Google's current Google Health app is a little clunky because it's trying to do a bunch of stuff at once, and it's not yet streamlined. Apple Health, on the other hand, was happy to give you information across a bunch of different metrics. It'll tell you where that information came from, which app is actually supplying it, which I found really cool.

I really, really, really liked its medication reminder feature! That's just such a *"duh!"* thing. Why doesn't Google Health have a medication reminder feature? It's ridiculous! Not only that, but I like the further medication information it gives you. You could enter in your medication and then check to see if it interacts with alcohol or other medications that you may take or are planning to take.

Apple Health also really integrated with all of the health apps, even Google Health, it was happy to take data from them and share data back, which I find great for a company that supposedly exists for other people. The Google Health app is a huge taker and never likes to share the data it has.
