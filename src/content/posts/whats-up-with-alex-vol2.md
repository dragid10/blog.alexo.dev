## A new bag

With the trip to Japan coming up in 3 months, my fiancé Maddy has been researching backpacks to wear out on our day trips (luckily I get to benefit from all of her research). She helped me find the [**Cotopaxi Todo 22L Convertible Tote**](https://www.cotopaxi.com/products/todo-22l-convertible-tote?variant=43327142264893). I really enjoy the idea of backpacks that can be tote bags because I've collected a lot of totes recently just because they're cool. Sometimes a backpack just feels too formal.

My issue with many tote-packs that I've seen is that it looks like a backpack that happens to be able to turn into a tote. Kinda like the manufacturer added the tote straps at the very end of the design process, so it looks awkward as one or the other. The Cotopaxi one that I just got, I think it looks good. It looks like a backpack and it looks like a tote. I have primarily been using it as a tote, and going between the two is actually pretty easy.

https://www.cotopaxi.com/products/todo-22l-convertible-tote?variant=43327142264893

After watching a lot of Naruto lately, it was actually a very joyful thing for me! Perhaps this is my opinion now because I already know what happens in the future of the series. I mean, was half of an episode basically just a recap of the end of the last episode? Yeah, it kind of was. These episodes are only like 20 minutes, so you really only got like 10, maybe 15 minutes' worth of new plot, with a lot of jump cuts and flashbacks.

Sometimes it was a little bit frustrating because I'm like, "Just get to the plot again! You're spending so much time on just this random flashback." I don't know how I did it as a kid. I got to relive a lot of the openings, which were always my favorite part of anime. The theme songs were so great!

I also got to experience a lot of episodes that I guess I happened to miss when I was a kid. A lot of episodes really did show the friendship between Naruto and Sasuke, because that always bewildered me. It never seemed like they were friends. It never seemed like Sasuke liked any of them, so why did Naruto call him his best friend? It always felt like a one-sided friendship, but I think they did establish it pretty well in the start of the series. It was quite a lot of fun to rewatch my main childhood anime.

## Something I'm excited for

I ordered the [**Pebble Index Ring**](https://repebble.com/index) from Pebble some time ago. It is a ring that you can use to record reminders for yourself. Perhaps you're thinking, "Alex, why do you need that? Just use your phone or remember." I have ADHD. If I could remember things, I would.

Also, the Pebble Index was very cheap. I think it was only $70. I know that I tend to easily misplace rings, and that is just a fact about myself, so I won't be horribly upset if I happen to lose this. I think the benefit that I would gain from it is actually pretty large. If you know me, you know that this is actually a huge problem that I've had, that I've tried to find a solution for, for years. I even bought a voice recorder to keep in my car so I can record voice notes of things: random thoughts I had, things to remember. The problem is I never remember to take the voice recorder out of my car in order to re-listen to the thing I've recorded.

A little smart ring on my finger that saves my transcript (whatever I'm saying to it) into the Pebble app sounds really, really cool to me, and that is what I'm excited for! I just got the notification that it shipped a couple days ago, so I'm eagerly looking forward to it.

## A random thought

I guess quick context about Naruto: he's a ninja. He's probably the hero. He's the protagonist. He's kind of the chosen one ninja. His signature move is summoning a shadow clone and doing one of his cool ninja moves. His shadow clones kind of poof out of existence when they take enough damage or when he decides to poof them himself.

There is this one scene that I watched where one of the enemies he was fighting was spitting nails directly at him, and Naruto summoned two or three shadow clones to just eat the nails, which meant they immediately got poofed. Ultimately, that gave Naruto the momentum to just punch his opponent because he was running at his opponent.

Something about that scene felt weird to me because whenever he summons shadow clones, it's typically to aid him in some battle he's having, and he's not just watching them die. They're typically doing something assisting along with his plan, maybe hiding him so he can pull off his ninja move in secret and deliver the final blow. Almost always, there are casualties with these shadow clones, but they are willing and active participants, right? They will dogpile someone, or they'll pretend to be the real him so that he can keep powering up his move.

In this instance, it just felt like they were summoned explicitly to die. The shadow clones have their own autonomy. They could do things of their own accord, and whenever they get poofed, all the information and knowledge that they have learned in their existence immediately transfers back to Naruto. Presumably, because they share his mind, they understand they're about to be summoned in order to take this attack and then immediately die afterwards. But it still feels weird because they don't get the choice to be summoned. Naruto chooses to summon them, so can they actually be considered willing participants in that? Summon, immediately die? That specific scene just felt a little bit odd. It felt a little bit unethical.

If you know Naruto, you understand that pretty much everything else in this show, the whole concept of the show, is that being a ninja is unethical no matter how cool and promising it seems. All the things they have to do are just horrible things. They are literal child soldiers.

This is one of the tiniest, tiniest problems with ethics in the show. This is very inconsequential compared to pretty much everything else that happens in the plot, but it's just something that made me pause and go, "Huh, I don't know. I don't know if that was a nice thing to do." That's it, that's the random thought. It was so stupid, but here we are!
