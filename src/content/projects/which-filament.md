Which Filament helps you work out which type of filament to use for your project. It's great for newcomers who don't know or understand all the different filament types yet. You can also browse by material, from PLA and PETG up to ABS, ASA and TPU, and open any of them to compare its variants like Rapid, Plus, Matte and Silk. Common examples such as a phone stand, a planter or a car mount each have a best pick, runners-up and what to avoid.

The site is open source and intentionally SUPER easy to contribute to!
