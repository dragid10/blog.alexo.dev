A Discord bot for people who run a regular D&D game and want less herding of players. The dungeon master configures it per server, and players use slash commands to RSVP to the next session, vote to cancel when the group is short, and skip a session. 
Its almost like sending a big group text, except the bot does it automatically for you and lets you know if everyone is available.
